Smartphone Buying Guide: What Matters Most

ARTIPHONERA Guide6 min read

Focus on battery, camera, software support, storage and size instead of comparing every specification on the box.

Start with your priorities

Decide whether camera, battery, gaming, compact size or long software support matters most.

Storage matters long term

Photos, apps and video can grow quickly. Buying a little more storage can be useful if you keep phones for several years.

Do not overpay for specs you will not use

The most expensive phone is not automatically the best fit for every user.
