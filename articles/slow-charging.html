Why Is My Phone Charging Slowly?

Slow charging can come from the power adapter, cable, charging port, temperature, power source, phone settings, battery condition or the phone intentionally reducing charge speed near a high state of charge.

Updated: August 20267 min read
On this page
  • Check charger and cable
  • Check temperature
  • Check the port
  • Understand fast-charging limits

Start with the charger and cable

A high-powered charger cannot deliver its full capability if the cable or phone does not support the same charging standard. Try the manufacturer-recommended charger and cable when possible, or a reputable compatible replacement with the right power rating.

The power source matters

A laptop USB port, older wall adapter or some car USB ports can provide much less power than a modern fast charger. If charging is slow only from one source, compare with a known-good wall charger.

Heat can reduce charging speed

Phones may deliberately slow charging when they are hot to protect the battery. Remove the phone from direct sunlight, stop demanding apps and let it cool naturally before testing again.

Check the charging port carefully

If the cable feels unusually loose or charging disconnects when the plug moves, the port may be dirty or damaged. Avoid inserting metal objects into the port. If you cannot safely inspect or clean it, use professional service.

Important: Do not use damaged, swollen or overheating batteries, chargers or cables. Stop using equipment that shows signs of physical damage.

Why charging often slows near 80–100%

Many phones reduce charging power as the battery becomes fuller. Some also use optimized charging features that intentionally pause or slow charging based on your routine. This can be normal behavior rather than a fault.

FAQ

Does a higher-watt charger always charge faster?

No. The phone controls how much power it accepts, and the charger, cable and phone must support compatible charging standards.

Can using the phone while charging make it slower?

Yes. Gaming, navigation, video calls and other demanding tasks consume power at the same time the charger is supplying it.

Why is wireless charging slower?

Wireless charging can lose more energy as heat and may deliver less power than a wired fast-charging setup, depending on the devices involved.
