Android Storage Cleanup Without Deleting Important Files

ARTIPHONERA Guide6 min read

A practical way to review large files, downloads, media and apps before deleting anything you may need later.

Find the biggest categories first

Photos, videos, offline media and large games often account for most storage use.

Review downloads carefully

Messaging apps and browsers can accumulate files over time. Check before deleting so you do not remove something important.

Leave free space

Keeping some free storage helps with updates, temporary files and normal system operation.
