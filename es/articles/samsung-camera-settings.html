Samsung Galaxy Camera Settings for Better Everyday Photos

ARTIPHONERA Guide6 min read

Simple Galaxy camera settings and habits that can improve everyday photos without turning photography into a technical project.

Start with the main camera

The main camera is usually the most reliable choice in mixed lighting. Use other lenses when the scene actually benefits from them.

Keep the lens clean

A fingerprint on the camera lens can reduce contrast and create haze, especially around lights.

Use higher resolution selectively

Higher resolution can help in bright conditions, but it also creates larger files and is not automatically better in every situation.
