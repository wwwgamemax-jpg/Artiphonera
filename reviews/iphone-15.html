Apple Review

iPhone 15 Review

A straightforward iPhone with USB-C, a capable camera system and familiar iOS experience.

8.5/ 10
ARTIPHONERA verdict

A strong option for users whose priorities match its software, size and feature set.

What we like

  • Strong everyday performance
  • High-quality display experience
  • Capable camera system
  • Modern software features

Things to consider

  • Price varies significantly by market
  • Some features depend on ecosystem preference
  • Storage needs differ by user

Design and display

iPhone 15 is designed as a modern smartphone for daily use. Screen quality, size and ergonomics should be considered together rather than judging the display from one specification.

Performance and software

For typical messaging, browsing, photography and apps, modern flagship performance is generally strong. The bigger decision is often which software ecosystem and interface you prefer.

Cameras

Camera quality depends on lighting, lens choice and processing. Consider the types of photos and videos you actually take instead of focusing only on megapixel counts.

Battery and charging

Real battery life changes with brightness, network conditions, camera use, gaming and background activity. Charging convenience can matter as much as peak charging specifications.

Who is it for?

A straightforward iPhone with USB-C, a capable camera system and familiar iOS experience.

Verdict

Consider this phone if its strengths line up with your priorities. Compare current pricing, storage and support in your region before buying.
